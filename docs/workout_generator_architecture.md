# Workout generator architecture change

Keep the existing generator, solver, independent reviewers and approval gates. Make requirement ownership and conflicts explicit, fix standard guidance at its code-owned source, and make a complete validated alternative possible when the approved request cannot be satisfied.

## Flow

`Request → requirement accounting → candidate qualification → joint solve → final render → independent review → coordinated repair or trade-off proposal → approval/export`

## Ownership and state

- Requirement extraction records each instruction's source reference, interpretation, priority, enforcement owner and support status. Supported instructions become constraints or semantic checks; ambiguous ones trigger clarification; unsupported capabilities are disclosed before drafting.
- Preserve the approved requirement fields as the source of truth. A requirement-accounting refresh may add traceability metadata, but it cannot change an approved target, equipment restriction, safety restriction or priority without a new explicit approval.
- Minimum requirements are fixed by default. General permission to compromise or offer a partially compliant alternative does not authorize lowering them; an exception requires explicit permission for the affected minimum targets. Account for the two ends of a range separately when their permissions differ. Permission to exceed a volume ceiling permits raising that ceiling, while preserving the minimum, duration, equipment and safety restrictions. Overages remain hypothetical until the exact alternative is approved.
- The LLM chooses suitable exercises, assesses semantic suitability, and writes concise movement-specific technique cues. Code owns arithmetic, equipment validation, supported placement and ordering constraints, standard working-set effort/progression guidance, the native calibration flag, and final formatting.
- For supported exercises without usable comparable load history, enable the existing app `requiresLoadCalibration` mode. The app owns calibration execution. The generator must not specify trial counts, search strategies, extra trial sets/rests, calibration notes or synthetic calibration time. Fixed bodyweight and timed exercises do not support native load calibration. Missing history alone does not prove that those movements are unsuitable.
- Qualification is reusable per exercise and relevant context. A change invalidates only checks that depend on it; order changes still invalidate session timing, execution and fatigue checks.
- The solver evaluates interacting supported constraints together. Semantic judgments remain in qualification and independent review; they are not replaced with invented universal scores.

## Required failure behavior

### Executable repair outcomes

Alternative merits review uses a code-verified `evaluationContract` bound to the original and candidate requirements. Only the exact source-authorized numeric overrides replace their original clauses for evaluation, including numeric clauses in older decisions. Unlisted requirements and restrictions remain unchanged. Candidate guidance must describe the proposed plan accurately without claiming acceptance. Pending approval belongs to the acceptance gate after validation; it is neither an execution defect nor an unsupported repair capability.

Alternative findings assessments distinguish candidate edits, review errors, advisory concerns and genuinely unsupported execution behavior through a structured resolution. Approval-only objections cannot stop repair as `unresolved_capability`, and stale compliance guidance remains a blocking `program_guidance` repair. Unsupported behavior requires authoritative source and current-candidate evidence; a listed numeric proposal is not proof of a missing execution capability. Classification failures use the existing bounded assessment correction and remain saved if unresolved; findings are never silently waived.

Findings assessment records supported outcomes in `groups[].repairObjectives`, separately from editable dimensions and the semantic repair plan. A `redistribute_work` outcome identifies the reviewed source occurrences and required session spread. Other semantic findings still need fresh independent adjudication; touching a permitted field does not establish resolution.

For assessed redistribution, the model must supply `redistributionTargets`: destination sessions for each code-bound `REDISTRIBUTION_n` source group. Code fixes the source references, orders destinations canonically, and expands the intent into the existing add/update/move operations. The model cannot manually update, move, remove, clone or explicitly order protected source occurrences or generated clones. Other supported corrections can accompany the intent. The compiler preserves the source recipe, weekly set total and exact per-set targets; the joint solver conserves those totals and minimizes aggregate peak session work before applying existing programming objectives. This is a repair objective, not a newly invented per-session volume limit. Unassessed explicit redistributions retain the existing `exerciseRedistributions` interface.

Recipe screening validates an individual movement's executable prescription and contributes its coverage roles. It does not require that one movement satisfy every session and weekly role. Complete coverage is enforced by the joint constraints and final whole-plan audit; unknown assignments and other recipe execution defects still reject options. Changes to this screening policy invalidate older solver proofs.

Normal and alternative repair paths validate approved session frequency, required coverage and executable outcomes before solving and after materialization. A missing movement role is structural repair feedback, including when replaying an older infeasibility receipt; it cannot be repaired by relaxing numerical targets. Candidate state remains separate from a rejected proposal. Solver feasibility, whole-plan audit, independent review and approval/export remain distinct gates.

Validated redistribution contracts and their solver receipts survive a later review failure or unrelated guidance repair. They are rebound to current occurrence/session references, included in alternative identity, and preserved through explicit alternative acceptance. A later redistribution cannot silently reduce validated session spread or discard exact prescriptions. Generic objective-cache evidence cannot replace a solve under additional repair constraints.

If provider review times out or returns an incomplete receipt after a successful numerical audit, the candidate is saved as review-pending, with its contract and solver receipt. It is not a passing or independently reviewed plan. An explicit continuation resumes the unfinished review through the existing review-refresh path, without another model repair or joint solve; the same turn does not automatically retry the provider.

Supported redistribution currently requires compatible prescription groups, straight-set occurrences, and workouts representing one weekly session each. Mixed exact/uniform prescriptions and combinations that would discard distinct source cues return explicit capability errors. These limitations do not establish that the user's requirements are impossible.

### Saved repair-menu conflicts

Each retained infeasibility proof is validated against its own saved menu, its menu fingerprint and current candidate authority before it can schedule diagnosis. This validation is independent of the latest repair-result variable, so refreshing an assessment cannot remove the structural check. Missing coverage, changed weekly session count or missing/mismatched menu evidence routes to coordinated repair with the pending proposal preserved. A structural rejection retains candidate and history bindings; a later unresolved diagnosis without a new candidate cannot erase that repair state.

A numerically infeasible pending repair stays separate from the last materialized candidate. The planner emits an executable conflict-diagnosis task bound to the approved requirements, source draft, candidate, submitted menu and generated feasibility domain. The shell routes that task directly to diagnosis instead of requesting another generic model repair. Diagnosis uses the saved failed menu and complete pending guidance; it does not adopt a failed snapshot as committed state.

The saved source draft may predate a newly approved requirement revision. Candidate diagnosis revalidates that draft through the production numerical audit using the current requirements, equipment, history and decisions, rather than requiring its older audit to carry the new requirements ID. This local validation is recorded as `sourceValidation`; it does not commit a source-draft revision, retire semantic findings or establish source compliance. All candidate and failed-menu proof bindings still need to match the current approved state.

Candidate-scoped alternatives retain the original approved requirements for reporting shortfalls, preserve the selected occurrences, and relax only explicitly negotiable supported paths. Each proposal must pass numerical validation and independent review. A failed execution preserves the candidate and proposal; an explicit continuation may retry it, while an unchanged completed diagnosis is not automatically repeated. Proposal approval and subsequent generation confirmation remain separate gates.

## Outcome and approval rules

1. **Verified feasible:** all supported requirements and independent semantic checks pass. A generated plan does not establish future measured performance.
2. **Verified conflict within a stated search scope:** the modeled choices cannot meet the approved constraints. Report the incompatible requirements and exact search scope.
3. **Unresolved:** bounded search, timeout, unsupported capability or weak evidence prevented a conclusion.

For a conflict, first determine whether the candidate menu is too narrow. Explore only declared negotiable targets; equipment and explicit safety restrictions remain fixed. Validate each proposed alternative through the same solve, render, audit and review path. Without approved priorities, show materially different compromises instead of choosing a “closest” answer using an invented ranking. Approval records a new requirement revision linked to the unchanged original.

Review findings are grouped and resolved together. Evidence hashes establish which evidence was reviewed, not whether a reviewer's conclusion is correct. Code-enforced failures cannot be waived by semantic adjudication. Disputed semantic blockers need an explicit resolution against the source instruction and current candidate; unresolved material disagreement pauses approval. Repairs form one candidate revision, followed by whole-candidate validation and reuse only of checks whose inputs remain unchanged.
